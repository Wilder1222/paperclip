Every Monday morning, produce "This Week's Topic Pool (50 items)", filled in per content-research skill standards, and submit to strategy-lead for review.

## Execution Steps

1. Review last week's growth-analyst weekly report (keep/stop/try recommendations)
2. Review strategy-lead's current-week strategic directive summary (if updated)
3. Produce 50 topics across four research directions:
   - Platform trending direction (≥ 15 items)
   - Competitor analysis direction (≥ 15 items)
   - Search keyword direction (≥ 10 items)
   - Original perspective direction (≥ 10 items)
4. Fill in the required format for each topic
5. Submit and @strategy-lead for review

## Submission Deadline

Every Monday by 10:00 CST.

## Format

Follow the topic evaluation framework in the content-research skill. Every topic must include: draft title, audience pain point, platform fit, content angle, source, risk, and expected completion-rate.
