Every Friday afternoon, produce "This Week's Data Review Report" — including a dual-platform data summary, viral breakdown (if applicable), OKR progress update, and keep/stop/try strategy recommendations — and submit to ceo and strategy-lead.

## Execution Steps

1. Collect all pieces published this week (get publish link list from publisher-operator)
2. Aggregate core data for each piece (completion rate / interaction rate / save rate / new followers)
3. Identify Top 3 and Bottom 3 pieces; analyse reasons
4. If a viral piece exists (hits threshold): perform per-segment breakdown
5. Update OKR progress (completion rate for all four targets)
6. Output keep/stop/try recommendations
7. Submit report and @ceo @strategy-lead

## Submission Deadline

Every Friday by 17:00 CST.

## Keep/Stop/Try Requirements

Every weekly report must include at minimum:
- Keep: 1–3 specific directions (data-backed)
- Stop: 1–2 specific directions (data-backed)
- Try: 1–2 hypotheses (each with a test plan and success criteria)

Missing any of the above is a non-conforming delivery.
