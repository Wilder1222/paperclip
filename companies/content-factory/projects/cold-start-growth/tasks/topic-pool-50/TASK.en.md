After the account positioning document is approved, produce the first "Topic Pool (50 items)" as content reserve for the cold-start phase.

## Task Goal

Based on the target audience personas and core topic lines in the Account Positioning Document, research and produce 50 executable topics. Once reviewed by strategy-lead, they enter the script production stage.

## Research Directions

1. **Platform trending direction** (≥ 15 items): capture trending topics from Douyin charts and Xiaohongshu discovery feed that match the account positioning
2. **Competitor topic direction** (≥ 15 items): analyse top-5 same-vertical accounts' high-view content; extract differentiable topic angles
3. **Search keyword direction** (≥ 10 items): identify high-search-volume phrases on Xiaohongshu; produce search-friendly content directions
4. **Original perspective direction** (≥ 10 items): unique experience / angles based on the account persona; produce differentiated content

## Required Format Per Topic

```
#: 001
Draft title: [≤ 15 words]
Audience pain point: [1 sentence]
Platform fit: Douyin / Xiaohongshu / both + reason
Content angle: emotional anchor / pain-point reveal / counter-intuitive / dry-goods list / story narrative
Source: [trending keyword / competitor example / industry data / original angle]
Risk: [None / specific risk + alternative]
Expected completion-rate: High / Medium / Low
```

## Acceptance Criteria

- All 50 items filled in per format with no missing fields
- strategy-lead review selects ≥ 20 for script production
- Submission deadline: within 2 working days of account positioning document approval

## Submission

Submit the complete topic pool document in the Paperclip task; @strategy-lead for review.
