Write the knowledge-IP account positioning document. This document will guide the entire production pipeline and must be approved by the CEO before serving as the baseline for all content production.

## Task Goal

Produce an "Account Positioning Document" that can guide the full pipeline; obtain CEO written approval via a Paperclip task comment.

## Required Sections

1. **Account Persona**
   - Who you are (professional background, domain expertise, experience tags)
   - Your relationship with the audience (friend / mentor / fellow traveller)
   - Tone and style (serious / light / sharp / healing)

2. **Target Audience Profile**
   - Demographics (age range, occupation, life stage)
   - Top-5 pain points (prioritised)
   - Top-5 desires (what they most want to achieve)
   - Content consumption habits (when, where, how long they watch)

3. **Content Positioning**
   - Core topic lines (2–3 long-term directions for sustained output)
   - Content no-go zones (topics / formats to never touch)
   - Differentiation positioning (main difference from competitors in the same vertical)

4. **Near-Term Growth Goals**
   - Within 90 days: follower accumulation + trust building
   - Content strategy: build vertical expertise and professional influence through consistent output
   - Engagement strategy: boost comment-section activity and build authentic connections with the audience

5. **Dual-Platform Strategy**
   - Douyin account positioning (focus: rapid recognition)
   - Xiaohongshu account positioning (focus: in-depth content and search traffic)

## Acceptance Criteria

- CEO written approval (Paperclip task comment)
- Document word count ≥ 800 words
- All required sections complete

## Submission Format

Submit as a document in the Paperclip task; CC trend-researcher (as baseline for topic research).
