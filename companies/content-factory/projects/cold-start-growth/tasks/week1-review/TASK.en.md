After all first-batch pieces are published (or 7 days after publishing begins), produce a "First-Week Review Report" including a data summary, viral breakdown, and keep/stop/try strategy recommendations.

## Task Goal

Based on real data from the first 10 pieces, produce a review report that can guide the second round of content production — completing the first "cold-start data → strategy iteration" feedback loop.

## Report Must Include

1. **Data Overview**
   - Douyin: X pieces published, total views X, average completion rate X%, new followers X
   - Xiaohongshu: X pieces published, total impressions X, average save rate X%, new followers X
   - Viral piece count (threshold-hit content)

2. **Top 3 Content Analysis** (best performers across all metrics)
   - Title, platform, core metrics, success reason analysis

3. **Bottom 3 Content Analysis** (worst performers across all metrics)
   - Title, platform, core metrics, failure reason analysis

4. **Viral Breakdown** (if a viral piece exists: per-segment analysis of the best piece)

5. **OKR Progress Initial Assessment**
   - Is the current follower growth rate on track to hit the 90-day 20 k target?
   - Is the current output cadence sufficient to reach 20 pieces per week?

6. **Keep/Stop/Try Strategy Recommendations**
   - Keep: [specific directions]
   - Stop: [specific directions]
   - Try: [next-batch test hypotheses]

## Submission Deadline

Day 7 after all first-batch pieces are published.

## After Submission

- Report submitted to ceo and strategy-lead
- strategy-lead updates account positioning document and topic standards based on review
- Launch content-factory-v1 project (pipeline standardisation)
