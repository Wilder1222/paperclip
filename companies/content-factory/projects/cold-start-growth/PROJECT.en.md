# Cold-Start Growth Project

This project handles the knowledge-IP account's cold start on Douyin and Xiaohongshu. The goal is to confirm account positioning, build a reserve of 50 topics, publish 10 pieces, and complete a data-driven first-week review — all within two weeks.

## Delivery Milestones

1. **M1 (Days 1–2)**: Account positioning document complete
2. **M2 (Days 3–4)**: Topic pool of 50 produced and approved
3. **M3 (Days 5–10)**: 10 pieces through the full production pipeline and published
4. **M4 (Days 11–14)**: First-week data review complete; iteration strategy delivered

## Success Criteria

- Account positioning document approved by CEO
- All 10 pieces published with first-comment operations completed on each
- First-week report includes keep/stop/try strategy recommendations
- At least 1 piece hits the viral threshold
