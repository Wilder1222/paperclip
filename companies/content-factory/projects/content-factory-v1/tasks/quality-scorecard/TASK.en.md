Produce a "Content Quality Scorecard" to establish a quantitative content quality standard for consistent evaluation in script review, production acceptance, and topic screening.

## Task Goal

Replace subjective judgement with quantified scoring to increase strategy-lead's review efficiency and allow script-writer and video-producer to self-check before submitting.

## Scorecard Must Include

### Script Quality Score (out of 100)
- Hook strength: 0–25 points (how attention-grabbing are the first 5 seconds / first slide?)
- Audience resonance: 0–25 points (how precisely does it hit a pain point / desire?)
- Content value density: 0–20 points (how concrete are the dry goods / case studies?)
- Engagement design: 0–15 points (how naturally is the engagement question embedded?)
- CTA conversion power: 0–15 points (how clearly does the call to action direct the next step?)

**Grade standards**:
- 90+ points: pass immediately; prioritise for publishing
- 75–89 points: pass with minor revisions
- 60–74 points: rewrite core sections
- Below 60 points: return for full redo

### Production Quality Score (out of 100)
- Cover click-attractiveness: 0–30 points
- Video / graphic visual quality: 0–25 points
- Subtitle / text legibility: 0–25 points
- Overall completion: 0–20 points

### Topic Quality Score (out of 100)
- Audience pain-point precision: 0–30 points
- Platform fit: 0–30 points
- Degree of differentiation: 0–20 points
- Compliance risk: 0–20 points

## Acceptance Criteria

- CEO approval
- All agents read and confirm "can self-check against this standard"
- strategy-lead trial-runs the scorecard for 2 rounds (≥ 10 pieces) and confirms effectiveness

## Submission Deadline

Within 2 working days of the prompt template library completion.
