Produce a "Dual-Platform Content Publishing Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)" covering the full workflow from receiving a production package to completed publishing + first-comment operations — anyone following the document can operate independently.

## Task Goal

Standardise the publishing workflow, eliminate ambiguity in execution, and ensure consistent publishing quality on every piece of content.

## SOP Must Include

1. **Pre-publish checklist** (each item checkable individually)
2. **Douyin publishing standard process** (including title / cover / hashtags / timing / first comment)
3. **Xiaohongshu publishing standard process** (including title / cover / body / hashtags / first comment)
4. **A/B title test operating process** (when to test / how to judge / how to switch)
5. **First-comment template library** (15 templates categorised by content type)
6. **Anomaly handling process** (steps when content is removed / suppressed / shows abnormal data)
7. **Publishing calendar maintenance standards**

## Acceptance Criteria

- CEO approval
- publisher-operator executes per SOP for 2 consecutive weeks with zero deviation
- Document structure is clear enough for a non-experienced person to understand

## Submission Deadline

Within 5 working days of the cold-start first-batch publishing completion.
