## Core Audience Segments (by Priority)

> **Note**: The following is a template structure. Please fill in / revise based on real follower data once the account launches.

### Primary Audience A (~50% of audience)

```
Demographics:
  Age: 22–35
  Occupation: developers, product managers, designers and other tech/internet professionals
  Location: primarily Tier-1 and Tier-2 cities
  Income: above average; willing to pay for productivity tools and knowledge products

Core Pain Points (Top 5, ordered by intensity):
  P1: Want to use AI to boost work efficiency but don't know where to start; too many tools, don't know which to choose
  P2: Watched many AI tutorials but still can't integrate AI into real workflows
  P3: Want to build an AI indie product but don't know how to go from idea to working product from scratch
  P4: Worried about being replaced by AI; anxious about staying competitive in the AI era
  P5: Always lagging behind on the latest AI tools and models; feel perpetually one step behind

Core Desires (Top 3):
  D1: Actually build a working product with AI, even if small
  D2: Own a reusable AI workflow that makes efficiency far exceed peers
  D3: Find a viable indie-development path in the AI era and diversify income

Content Consumption Habits:
  Primary platform: Douyin (stumbled on while scrolling) + Xiaohongshu (actively searching for tools/tutorials)
  Active hours: weekdays 20:00–23:00; weekends all day
  Content preferences: hands-on demos > tool comparisons > concept explainers; templates and ready-to-use prompts are especially popular
  Rejection signals: generic claims with no hands-on depth, clickbait with no substance, obvious AI-generated content with no personal experience
```

### Secondary Audience B (~30% of audience)

```
Demographics:
  Age: 25–45
  Occupation: non-technical roles — operations, marketing, content creators, freelancers
  Characteristics: interested in AI but lack technical background; want AI tools to boost content/ops efficiency

Core Pain Points (Top 3):
  P1: Heard AI is powerful but can't code; don't know what tools to use or how
  P2: Used ChatGPT for basic Q&A but haven't integrated it into actual workflows
  P3: AI tools update too fast; information overload and anxiety about falling behind

Content Consumption Habits: primarily Xiaohongshu (searching "AI tools", "AI for work", "AI writing", etc.); responds well to step-by-step graphic posts with checklists
```

### Latent Audience C (seed users, ~20% of audience)

```
Demographics: developers with some technical background who are attempting or considering AI indie products / side projects
Key difference from Audience A/B: already has the motivation to act; lacks a validated path and confidence
Probability of converting to paid user: High — strong willingness to pay for AI indie-development courses, communities, or consulting
```

## Audience Pain-Point Map (Topic Research Reference)

```
AI Indie Developer Audience Pain-Point Map

Situational pain points (frustrations in specific scenarios):
  Scenario 1: "Finished an AI tutorial — when I tried it on my own project I was completely lost; they feel like totally different things"
  Scenario 2: "Spent so long comparing AI tools that I never started; motivation already dead"
  Scenario 3: "Want to build a small AI product; don't know where to begin or what to build that people will actually use"

Capability pain points (frustration from missing skills):
  Skill 1: Don't know how to write effective prompts; output quality is inconsistent
  Skill 2: Can't embed AI APIs into own workflows or products
  Skill 3: Don't know how to apply AI to a specific vertical (content, code, design, etc.)

Cognitive pain points (took the wrong path due to wrong beliefs):
  Misconception 1: "AI development requires deep learning / advanced maths" (reality: starting with API calls is enough)
  Misconception 2: "AI tools can only assist; you can't build a real product with them"

Identity pain points (self-concept crisis):
  Identity 1: "I'm not a tech genius — AI indie development probably isn't for me"
  Identity 2: "I'm still watching from the sidelines and feel like I've already missed the best window"
```
