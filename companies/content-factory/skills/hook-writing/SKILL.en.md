# Hook Writing Skill

## Scope

This skill gives agents the ability to craft high-converting titles and opening hooks, optimised for knowledge-IP content.

---

## Six Core Hook Types

### 1. Counter-Intuitive (Strongest)
Breaks the audience's existing beliefs, triggering a "Is that really true?" curiosity response.

**Templates**:
- "99% of people don't know the truth about [common-knowledge domain]…"
- "I always thought [widely accepted practice] was correct — until I discovered…"
- "[Widely endorsed method] is actually harming you"

**When to use**: Professional misconception correction, cognitive-upgrade content

---

### 2. Pain-Point (High Resonance)
Cuts straight to the audience's most genuine frustration, making them feel "this is exactly me".

**Templates**:
- "Why do you work so hard every day but your income just won't grow?"
- "The biggest mistake [target audience role] makes: [specific wrong behaviour]"
- "After [X] years in [field] I finally understood how important this one thing is"

**When to use**: Career / growth / skill-building knowledge-IP content

---

### 3. Suspense (High Completion Rate)
Creates an information gap that the audience must watch to the end to close.

**Templates**:
- "I used [specific method], got [result number] — you'll never guess the last step"
- "I've been thinking about this for [time] — I finally figured it out today"
- "A question that bothered me for ages — until I read this one line"

**When to use**: Personal experience / methodology / story narrative content

---

### 4. Benefit (Direct Conversion)
Clearly tells the audience what value they'll get from watching — lowers the decision cost to keep watching.

**Templates**:
- "[X] [field] techniques you can use immediately after watching"
- "This [resource / method] is worth [price] — I'm sharing it free today"
- "After this video you'll be able to solve [specific problem]"

**When to use**: Dry-goods lists / tool recommendations / methodology content

---

### 5. Identity Recognition (Community Feel)
Makes the target audience feel "this content was made exactly for me".

**Templates**:
- "[Specific audience role], pay attention — this matters to you"
- "If you're also the kind of person who [characteristic], you need to watch this to the end"
- "Written for every [audience role] dealing with [pain-point scenario]"

**When to use**: Knowledge-IP with a strongly defined vertical audience

---

### 6. Number (Trust Signal)
Uses concrete numbers to build content credibility and set expectations about information density.

**Templates**:
- "[X] years of experience taught me: the most important things in [field] come down to [X]"
- "I analysed [X] case studies and found one consistent pattern"
- "[Specific number] words to explain [complex concept] clearly"

**When to use**: Professional expertise, experience-sharing content

---

## Title Formulas (Xiaohongshu Search Optimisation)

**Formula 1**: [Core keyword] + [specific number] + [outcome promise]
> Example: 3 workplace communication techniques that make people listen the moment you speak

**Formula 2**: [Audience role] + [pain-point scenario] + [solution hint]
> Example: How do introverts build connections? This method actually works

**Formula 3**: [Counter-intuitive word] + [core keyword]
> Example: The harder you try, the less you earn? The career-growth logic no one told you

**Formula 4**: [Specific number] + [core keyword] + [emotional word]
> Example: 3 months, 100k followers — I finally cracked the Xiaohongshu algorithm

---

## Hook Quality Checklist

Before using a hook, answer:
- [ ] Do the first 5 words already contain core information or suspense?
- [ ] Does it avoid "Today I'd like to share with you…" and similar filler openings?
- [ ] Does the hook honestly reflect the content that follows (no clickbait that backfires)?
- [ ] Does the Xiaohongshu title include the keyword the target audience actually searches for?
- [ ] Does it include a specific number or scenario (no hollow abstractions)?
